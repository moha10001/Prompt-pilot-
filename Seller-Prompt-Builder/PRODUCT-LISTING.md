# Product Listing — Seller Prompt Builder

## Product Name
**Seller Prompt Builder** — AI Prompt Builder for Digital Product Sellers

## Tagline
Build structured, marketplace-ready AI prompts for research, listings, SEO, positioning and launch — in your browser. No API key. No subscription. No fluff.

## Short Description (Gumroad / Etsy / Shopify)
Stop writing weak prompts from scratch.

Seller Prompt Builder is a zero-dependency browser tool that turns your product idea into clear, structured AI prompts for digital-product sellers on Etsy, Shopify, Gumroad and beyond.

Choose a workflow → fill a few fields → get a ready-to-paste prompt. Copy or download as TXT. Works offline.

- Competitor analysis

## FAQ Ideas (for listing)
**Do I need an AI subscription?**  
No. The tool builds the prompt. You paste it into any AI you already use (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.).

**Does it call the internet?**  
No. It runs entirely in your browser.

**Can I use it for client work?**  
Yes — for your own business and client work. Do not resell the unchanged package as a free public download.

**Is this a list of prompts?**  
No. It generates structured prompts based on *your* product, customer and goal.
